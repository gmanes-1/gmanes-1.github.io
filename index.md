# Giuseppe Manes

<p class="lead">
  I am a PhD student in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.irit.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IRIT</a> (Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse), at Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier, and a member of the <a href="https://www.irit.fr/departement/intelligence-artificielle/adria/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ADRIA</a> research team.
</p>

<p class="intro">
  My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, formal reasoning and uncertainty, with a particular interest in Possibility Theory. I am interested in how formal approaches to uncertainty can inform learning systems.
</p>

<p class="intro">
  Before joining IRIT, I studied Logic at the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC) at the University of Amsterdam, after completing a BA in Philosophy at the University of Padua and spending a semester at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.
</p>

<p class="intro supervision">
  <strong>Doctoral supervision</strong> · <a href="https://www.irit.fr/~Henri.Prade/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Henri Prade</a>, Florence Dupin de Saint-Cyr, and <a href="https://ismailbaaj.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ismaïl Baaj</a>.
</p>
</section>
