# Giuseppe Manes

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [IRIT](https://www.irit.fr/) (Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse), at University of Toulouse. I am a member of the [ADRIA](https://www.irit.fr/departement/intelligence-artificielle/adria/) research team. My PhD research is supervised by [Henri Prade](https://www.irit.fr/~Henri.Prade/), [Florence Dupin de Saint-Cyr](https://www.irit.fr/~Florence.Bannay/), and [Ismaïl Baaj](https://ismailbaaj.fr).

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, formal reasoning and uncertainty, with a particular interest in Possibility Theory. I am interested in how formal approaches to uncertainty can inform learning systems.

Before joining IRIT, I was at the [ILLC](https://www.illc.uva.nl) (Institute for Logic, Language and Computation) at the University of Amsterdam, where I completed the Master of Logic. There, under the supervision of [Ronald de Haan](https://staff.science.uva.nl/r.dehaan/) and [Mina Young Pedersen](https://minayoungp.xyz/index.html), I wrote a Master's thesis entitled [_Updating Uncertain Evidence: Conditionalization and Complexity in Multi-Layer Belief Models_](https://eprints.illc.uva.nl/id/document/13799). The thesis investigates how new uncertain evidence can be incorporated into Multi-Layer Belief Models, which combine belief functions and topological structures. It introduces a conditional belief and update procedure and examines its implications for belief revision and computational complexity.
