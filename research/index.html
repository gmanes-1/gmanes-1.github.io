Academic profile · Toulouse

Research

My work explores the connections between machine learning, formal reasoning and uncertainty.

My PhD research investigates connections between Machine Learning and Possibility Theory. I am interested in principled ways of representing and managing uncertainty in artificial intelligence, while keeping an eye on the computational costs of learning and reasoning.

Research interests

Machine Learning and Possibility Theory

Exploring possibility-theoretic approaches to uncertainty in learning systems.

Formal reasoning methods

Logic-based and mathematical approaches to reasoning in artificial intelligence.

Epistemic logic

Formal representations of knowledge, belief and information change.

Theory of computation

Foundational questions about computation and computational reasoning.

Research group

I am part of the ADRIA team (Argumentation, Décision, Raisonnement, Incertitude et Apprentissage) at IRIT.
