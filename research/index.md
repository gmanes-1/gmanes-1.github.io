My PhD research investigates the connections between **Machine Learning** and **Possibility Theory**. It aims to explore how Possibility Theory can be used to represent and manage uncertainty in artificial intelligence, while considering the computational costs of learning and reasoning, with the goal of developing efficient machine learning algorithms.

Aside, the theme of my current research my broader acadmic interests are are

- **Epistemic logics**
- **Theories of uncertainty** and **epistemic probability** (belief functions, possibility theory, ranking functions)
- **Theory of computation** (algorithmic randomness, computability theory and complexity theory)
