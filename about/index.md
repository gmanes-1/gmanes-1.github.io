I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [IRIT](https://www.irit.fr/) (Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse), at Université Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier. I am a member of the [ADRIA](https://www.irit.fr/departement/intelligence-artificielle/adria/) research team.

Before joining IRIT, I studied Logic at the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC) at the University of Amsterdam, after completing a BA in Philosophy at the University of Padua and spending a semester at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.

My academic interests sit between formal reasoning, logic and artificial intelligence. I am especially interested in how formal approaches to uncertainty can inform learning systems.

## Doctoral supervision

My PhD research is supervised by Henri Prade, Florence Dupin de Saint-Cyr, and Ismaïl Baaj.
