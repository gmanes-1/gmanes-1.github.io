I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [IRIT](https://www.irit.fr/) (Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse), at University of Toulouse. I am a member of the [ADRIA](https://www.irit.fr/departement/intelligence-artificielle/adria/) research team. My PhD research is supervised by [Henri Prade](https://www.irit.fr/~Henri.Prade/), [Florence Dupin de Saint-Cyr](https://www.irit.fr/~Florence.Bannay/), and [Ismaïl Baaj](https://ismailbaaj.fr).

Before joining IRIT, I studied Logic at the [ILLC](https://www.illc.uva.nl) (Institute for Logic, Language and Computation) at the University of Amsterdam, after completing a BA in Philosophy at the University of Padua and spending a semester at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.

My academic interests sit between formal reasoning, logic and artificial intelligence. I am especially interested in how formal approaches to uncertainty can inform learning systems.
